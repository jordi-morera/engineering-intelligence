# Skill: Specification Validation

## Purpose
Validate a specification: confirm evidence is sound, requirements are testable, and critical unknowns are resolved.

## When to use
After critique, before a specification can be marked READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION.

## Expected inputs
- A draft specification, its critiques, and the underlying evidence.

## Expected outputs
- A validation verdict: READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION, or BLOCKED with reasons.

## Important rules
- Do not advance to READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION with critical unknowns or contradictions.
- Confidence must be stated explicitly.
- Record who/what validated and when.

## Failure / uncertainty
- If any critical uncertainty remains, the correct outcome is BLOCKED, not optimism.

# Skill: Specification Writing

## Purpose
Produce an Engineering Specification document conforming to the schema.

## When to use
When research and analysis for a work item are complete enough to draft a specification.

## Expected inputs
- Requirements or root cause, research findings, technical context, assumptions, open questions.

## Expected outputs
- A structured specification following `schemas/engineering-spec.yaml`.

## Important rules
- Adhere to the schema structure.
- Keep facts, assumptions, and unknowns clearly separated.
- Every claim should reference evidence where possible.
- Do not invent details to fill gaps; record them as open questions.

## Failure / uncertainty
- If the specification cannot be completed honestly, leave the relevant section blank or flagged as blocked.

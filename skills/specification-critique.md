# Skill: Specification Critique

## Purpose
Critically review a specification to surface gaps, contradictions, and unsupported assumptions.

## When to use
After a specification draft is created and before final validation.

## Expected inputs
- A draft specification and its underlying evidence.

## Expected outputs
- A list of critiques, each with a disposition (accepted / rejected) applied to the specification.

## Important rules
- Adopt a skeptical second-pass mindset.
- Look for: unsupported claims, untested assumptions, contradictions, missing acceptance criteria, unresolved open questions.
- Every critique should be actionable, not vague.

## Failure / uncertainty
- Be willing to mark the specification BLOCKED if critiques reveal critical gaps.
